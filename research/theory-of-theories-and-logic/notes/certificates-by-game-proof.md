## 0. The organising principle (the "theory of theories" for Theorem-R methods)

A game is a triple (letter group, free subgroup G, relations). A certificate is a representation sigma of the letter
group that kills the relations, plus a subspace Y kept by sigma(G); its value is f(K)/f(D). Three invariants decide
which certificates exist and how far they can go:

1. **The forced kernel.** The relations of the game force sigma to kill a normal subgroup. Engine game: nothing
   (any representation of the Clifford group). Column mixing with P: every column-uniform Pauli and i^f (ut-direct
   Prop C), so sigma factors through Sp(2m,2). Column mixing plus one non-Clifford reshuffle: the whole one-column
   Clifford group (Theorem 2 below), so nothing is left.
2. **The frame geometry.** If the stabiliser of the base frame is exactly G (times what sigma kills), every
   certificate is a subspace family on the homogeneous space letter-group/G, and its value is a ratio of averages over
   two orbitals (Theorem 1). Engine: the dual polar graph DSp(2m,2). Chain maps of prefix-XOR type at the F_4 symbol:
   the Hermitian dual polar graph of H(2m-1,4) (27 frames at m = 2, 891 at m = 3).
3. **Multiplicities.** On a multiplicity-free module a certificate is a cut (a sum of pieces) and its value is bounded
   by spectral (Delsarte) data of the orbital scheme (Proposition 3). Only modules where some piece of G occurs twice can
   carry "label" certificates (pf-duality Lemma 1.5(c)), and those are the only ones that can beat every cut.

Two barriers sit on top (both already upheld): every proof that a family's ceiling is below m must use characteristic 0
(pf-duality Corollary C, the mod-2 frame family is perfect), and no rank method can exclude a target that has an
approximate (border) scheme (pf-ladder B1).

## 1. Theorem 1: in the engine game, Pauli-blindness costs nothing [proved]

**Setting.** Block m >= 2. Gamma_e = group generated by the engine's letter matrices (free: Paulis, affine address
maps, the engine's Z4 phase letters; paid: directions and blocks); pi: Gamma_e -> Sp(2m,2) the action on Pauli labels;
P = stabiliser of L_0 = {(0|z)} in Sp(2m,2); S = pi^-1(P).
Facts used, all upheld: (A) pi is onto Sp(2m,2) and the free letters map onto P; (B) the only scalars in Gamma_e are
+-1, +-i, they and the Paulis are free letters, so S = G_e (the group generated by the free letters)
[v-pf-induction-logic, Facts A and B, in `../../proofs/DONE-13oct.json`]; Sp(2m,2) is distance-transitive on
Lagrangians, so P-double cosets in Sp(2m,2) correspond to the distance d(pi(h)L_0, L_0) [w-bounds s1 for m = 3;
v-pf-induction-logic for all m].

**Theorem 1.** Let (rho, N) be any engine certificate: rho a representation of Gamma_e on X over a field of
characteristic 0, N kept by every free letter. Then there is a certificate (tau, Y^) whose representation factors
through pi (so every Pauli and every scalar acts as the identity: it is *Pauli-blind*) with
f_{Y^}(g) = |Sp(2m,2)| f_N(g) for every g in Gamma_e. Hence the best value of Pauli-blind certificates equals the best
value of engine certificates, for single directions and for grouped blocks alike.

*Proof.*
(i) f_N(a h b) = f_N(h) for a, b in G_e (pf-duality Lemma 1.1: N is kept by a and b). If pi(h) and pi(h') lie in the
same P-double coset, write pi(h) = p pi(h') p'; choose s in pi^-1(p), s' in pi^-1(p'); then h = s h' s' k with
k in ker pi, and ker pi (Paulis and scalars) lies in S = G_e. So f_N(h) = f_N(h'): f_N(h) = phi(d) depends only on
d = d(pi(h)L_0, L_0).
(ii) For each Lagrangian U choose gamma_U in Gamma_e with pi(gamma_U)L_0 = U and put Y_U = rho(gamma_U)N. Another choice
differs by an element of S on the right, which keeps N, so Y_U is well defined. Applying rho(gamma_V)^-1,
delta(U,V) := dim(Y_U + Y_V) - dim Y_V = f_N(gamma_V^-1 gamma_U) = phi(d(U,V)), because pi(gamma_V^-1 gamma_U) maps L_0
to pi(gamma_V)^-1 U, which is at distance d(U,V) from L_0.
(iii) Averaging Lemma (w-bounds s3) for the group Sp(2m,2) acting on frames: tau on lists (v_gamma)_{gamma in Sp(2m,2)}
with values in X, (g.v)_gamma = v_{pi(g)^-1 gamma}; Y^ = {v : v_gamma in Y_{gamma^-1 L_0}}. tau factors through pi. A free
letter g has pi(g) L_0 = L_0, so g.Y^ = Y^. Position by position, f_{Y^}(g) = sum_gamma delta(gamma^-1 pi(g) L_0,
gamma^-1 L_0) = |Sp(2m,2)| phi(d(pi(g)L_0, L_0)) = |Sp(2m,2)| f_N(g). QED

## 2. Theorem 2: reshuffles plus chain maps leave no product-form certificate [proved]

**Setting.** The column-mixing game of ut-direct s4 (chi = row ^= row << 1, E = row0 ^= row1 << 1, column-uniform S on
row 1 and CZ, hence P = i^(parity of row 1)), plus the engine's column-uniform letters, plus ONE column-uniform
non-Clifford reshuffle that the widened game makes free: the Toffoli (m >= 3) or the controlled-S phase
CS = i^(x_0 x_1) (m >= 2; a Z4 phase pattern). Product-form certificate: sigma(F) = beta(F) (x) F_f with
Y = Y_0 (x) H_f, as in Theorem R-infinity, Theorem B and Theorem E (ut-direct s2, s4.3; ut-normal s3, s4).

**Theorem 2.** For every such certificate, f_sigma(K) = 0 (and f_sigma(D_z) = 0 at m >= 3): it proves nothing, at
every block size m >= 2.

*Proof.* By ut-direct Prop C (and s4.3 for exact product form), sigma kills every column-uniform Pauli family and i^f.
One-column identities [computed, `nogo_check.py`]: Tof X_0 Tof^-1 = X_0 CNOT(1->2) and CS X_0 CS^-1 = X_0 S_1 CZ(0,1);
taken column-uniformly they hold at every f. Since sigma(X_0^(x)f) = 1, sigma(CNOT(1->2)^(x)f) = 1 (Toffoli case) or
sigma((S_1 CZ)^(x)f) = 1 (CS case). The column-uniform Clifford families form a group isomorphic to the one-column
Clifford group C_m (g -> (g^(x)f)_f is injective), and the kernel of sigma restricted to it is a normal subgroup that
contains the Paulis and the scalars, so it is pi^-1 of a normal subgroup of Sp(2m,2) containing q := pi(killer) != 1.
m >= 3: Sp(2m,2) is simple [standard; cited from memory: Dickson; e.g. D. E. Taylor, The Geometry of the Classical
Groups (1992); R. A. Wilson, The Finite Simple Groups (2009) s3.5], so the normal closure of q is everything
[at m = 3 also computed, `out_nogo_check.txt`: the images generate a group of order 1451520 = |Sp(6,2)|, and the
normal closure of q(CNOT(1->2)) (a class of 315 elements; 5 conjugates suffice) is the whole group]; sigma is
trivial on C_m, in particular sigma(D_z) = sigma(K) = 1 and f_sigma(D_z) = f_sigma(K) = 0.
m = 2 (CS case) [computed, `out_nogo_check.txt`]: the images generate Sp(4,2) (order 720); the normal closure of
q(S_1 CZ) has order 360 (index 2) and contains q(K) = q(D_0 D_1). So sigma(K) = 1 and f_sigma(K) = 0. QED

**Remark (single f).** sigma = evaluation at one large f is a genuine representation of the family algebra (ut-normal
Lemma 1) and is not covered by Theorem 2; but with a chain map free no subspace of H_f other than 0 and H_f is kept by
all free letters (g-hypotheses, computed at block 2 for f = 2..5 and block 3 for f = 3; proved for every f at block 2 by
its skeptic), so it is empty too. Twisted subspaces N of U_0 (x) H_f (not of the form Y_0 (x) H_f) with a nontrivial
beta are covered by no existing theorem (Theorem R-infinity's end count needs the form Y_0 (x) H_f): that is the only
door left for Theorem-R-type methods in this game [argued].

**What survives.** Only certificates that are not of product form. The one known is ut-direct s6's 2-adic lattice
potential (one f, any monomial cheap letters, 2-integral mixing): 1.5W at block 2 and 1.875W at block 3 (f = 2),
sqrt(m) W in the limit. So in this game nothing we have excludes any unit with M/W >= 1.5 (block 2) or >= 1.875
(block 3), e.g. 2 in 3 at block 2 (0.415) and 3 in 8 at block 3. This is where certificates provably cannot follow;
it is the game in which a construction search is not already known to be futile.

## 3. Proposition 3: coordinate (colouring) frame families are capped below 2 [proved]

**Proposition 3.** On a distance-regular frame graph (dual polar graph of rank m) with eigenmatrix P_d(j), every family
in which each Y_U is 0 or a line (in particular every 0/1 cut and every colouring), and by the mediant every direct
sum of such families (every "coordinate" family: Y_U spanned by vectors of one fixed basis), has
abar_m/abar_1 <= max_{j>=1} (1 - P_m(j)/k_m)/(1 - P_1(j)/k_1).
*Proof.* Let C_1, ..., C_r be the classes of frames with the same nonzero line and C_0 those with Y_U = 0. Then
delta(U,V) = 1 exactly when U is in some C_i (i >= 1) and V is not, so N k_d abar_d = sum_{i>=1} 1_{C_i}^T (k_d I - A_d)
1_{C_i} = sum_{i>=1} sum_{j>=1} (k_d - P_d(j)) ||E_j 1_{C_i}||^2 with E_j the eigenprojections (the j = 0 term vanishes).
Both numerator and denominator are nonnegative combinations of the same weights, so the ratio is at most the largest
termwise ratio. Direct sums: pf-duality Lemma 1.2. QED
**Values [computed exactly, `dpg_spectral.py` -> `out_dpg_spectral.txt`]:** engine frames DSp(2m,2): 3/2, 7/4, 15/8,
31/16, 63/32, 127/64 for m = 2..7 (= 2 - 2^(1-m); it agrees with pf-induction's best cut ratio kappa_m and is reached at
m = 3 by w-bounds' certificate A, 1.75). Hermitian frames H(2m-1,4) (chi game at the F_4 symbol): 5/4, 21/16, 85/64,
341/256 for m = 2..5 (tending to 4/3). For rank-2 targets (spectator game, m = 3): abar_2/abar_1 <= 3/2.
The Hermitian value 21/16 = 1.3125 at m = 3 is exactly the ceiling that xc3-cert3's fam22.py met with thin
P-invariant families on the 891 frames ("ratios <= 1.3125"): Proposition 3 says no colouring family there can do better.
**Consequence:** no coordinate frame family can ever close any open case listed in TABLE.md (all need > 2 at blocks
3-6, > 1.5 at block 2 in the chi game, > 13/7 for 7 in 13). Every useful certificate needs labels of dimension >= 2 in
non-distributive position (lifts, hook shadows).
