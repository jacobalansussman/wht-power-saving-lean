## 1. Theorem M (a certificate that excludes the block-3 mixed units in the engine game) [proved, given the results cited]

**Statement.** Let X be the irreducible representation of Sp(6,2) of degree 105 with value −35 on transvections (the
character 105a of the ATLAS table "S6(2)"), over C. Let A ≅ Sym(3,2) ≅ F_2^6 be the unipotent radical of P_0 = Stab(L0) and
O28 the GL(3,2)-orbit of size 28 on the characters of A. Put

    N = ⊕_{ψ ∈ O28} X_ψ      (X_ψ = {x : a x = ψ(a) x for all a in A}).

Then N is kept by P_0, dim N = 56, and with h = t_(e1|e1), h2 = h·t_(e2|e2), k = h2·t_(e3|e3):

    (ψ1, ψ2, ψ3) = (f_N(h), f_N(h2), f_N(k)) = (20, 38, 49),   f_N(g) = dim(N + gN) − dim N.

Consequently ψ3 + 4ψ2 − 10ψ1 = +1 and 7ψ3 + 38ψ2 − 88ψ1 = +27, so in the engine game (and for every game whose
certificates factor through Sp(6,2) in the same way) no unit gives 1 kernel + 4 rank-2 targets with 10 rank-1 moves,
2 + 8 in 20, 4 + 21 in 49, or 7 + 38 in 88.

**Proof.**

(a) *The setting.* Labels (x|z) ∈ F_2^6, form x·z' + x'·z, L0 = {(0|z)}, M0 = {(x|0)}. The matrices of h, h2, k
(`sp6.py`, output printed when run) swap x_i ↔ z_i for i ≤ 1, 2, 3 respectively, i.e. they are the conjugation actions of
H_1, H_1H_2 and K on Pauli labels. h L0, h2 L0, k L0 lie at distance 1, 2, 3 from L0 and k L0 = M0 [computed, sp6.py].
A is generated by the six commuting transvections t_(0|w), w ∈ {e1, e2, e3, e1+e2, e1+e3, e2+e3} (their symmetric
matrices w wᵀ span Sym(3,2)); the Levi GL(3,2) acts by (x|z) ↦ (a x | a^{-T} z). Both keep L0.

(b) *N is kept by P_0.* A acts on X_ψ by the scalar ψ(a). For l in the Levi, l X_ψ = X_{l·ψ} with
(l·ψ)(a) = ψ(l^{-1} a l), and l·ψ ∈ O28 when ψ ∈ O28. P_0 = A ⋊ GL(3,2). ∎(b)

(c) *An exact integral model.* M = Ind_B^G(λ), B the stabiliser of the flag ⟨(0|e1)⟩ ⊂ ⟨(0|e1),(0|e2)⟩ ⊂ L0 (a Sylow
2-subgroup of P_0, order 512, B/B' ≅ F_2^4), λ the linear character with index 3 in `fgcore.py`'s coordinates. G acts
on Z^2835 (one basis vector per flag) by signed permutation matrices. M ⊗ C decomposes as
56 + 70 + 2·315 + 189b + 105a + 280a + 210b + 378 + 405 + 512 (GAP's induced character, `gap/out_borel.txt`, and the
eigenspace dimensions, `out_exact_cert2.txt`); 105a occurs once. The class sums Z2a (63 transvections) and Z2b (315
elements t_u t_v t_{u+v}, u, v spanning a totally isotropic line) act on the χ-isotypic part by the integers
z2a = 63χ(2a)/χ(1), z2b = 315χ(2b)/χ(1); for 105a these are (−21, 3), and no other constituent of M has this pair
(`out_exact_cert2.txt`, line "constituents"). Hence X = ker(Z2a + 21) ∩ ker(Z2b − 3) inside M ⊗ Q, exactly.

(d) *Integer vectors in X.* u := Σ_g χ(g) ρ(g) e_F = (|G|/χ(1)) E_χ e_F is an integer vector (χ is integer valued). It was
read off a floating-point projection times 13824 and rounded (rounding error 1e-11), and then CHECKED EXACTLY in integer
arithmetic: (Z2a + 21)u = 0 and (Z2b − 3)u = 0, u ≠ 0. So u ∈ X. The vectors ρ(g)u (g ∈ G, exact signed permutations)
lie in X; 105 of them, B = [ρ(g_1)u, …, ρ(g_105)u], have rank 105 mod p = 2^31 − 1, hence rank 105 over Q, so B is a
basis of X. A set R of 105 coordinates on which B_R is invertible mod p (hence over Q) was chosen: restriction to R is
injective on X, so for any integer matrix Y with columns in X, rank_Q Y = rank_Q Y_R.

(e) *N exactly.* 64·Π_ψ = Π_i (1 ± ρ(a_i)) is an integer operator. The columns of 64·Π_ψ B (ψ ∈ O28) are integer vectors
in X_ψ ⊂ N. Mod p, the 64 weight spaces of X have dimensions summing to 105 = dim X; since a mod-p rank never exceeds
the rational rank and the rational dimensions also sum to 105, every mod-p dimension is exact; the 28 spaces in O28 give
56. A set of 56 of these columns, independent mod p, therefore spans N = X^{O28} exactly. (Character-level check: X|P_0 =
14 + 14 + 21 + 56 with the 56-piece the only one of the O28 family, `gap/out_ctbl.txt`.)

(f) *The ranks.* For g ∈ {h, h2, k}, ρ(g) is an exact signed permutation; [N, ρ(g)N]_R is a 105 × 112 integer matrix.
Its rank over Q was computed by fraction-free (Bareiss) elimination with an exact-division assertion at every step
(`exact_cert.py`), and again by plain Gaussian elimination over Q with Python Fractions (`verify_exact.py`); both give
rank N_R = 56 and ranks 76, 94, 105, i.e. (ψ1, ψ2, ψ3) = (20, 38, 49). The ranks mod 2^31 − 1 agree.
[`out_exact_cert2.txt`, `out_verify_exact.txt`, matrices in `exact_mats_lam3.json`.] ∎(f)

(g) *From the certificate to the units.* Fact (A) of `../reps/PROOF.md` §1 (upheld: v-pf-induction-logic): the action on
Pauli labels π: Γ_e → Sp(6,2) is onto and every free letter of the engine maps into P_0. So ρ_X ∘ π is a representation
of the engine's letter group over C and N is kept by every free letter: (ρ_X ∘ π, N) is a Theorem-R certificate. (Theorem 1
of reps is not needed; it only says that nothing is lost by working with such Pauli-blind certificates.) π(H_1) = h,
π(H_1H_2) = h2, π(K) = k by (a), and f_N(g) depends only on the P_0 double coset of π(g) (pf-duality Lemma 1.1), so every
rank-1 paid move costs f_N(h) = 20 and a rank-2 target on any two of the three rows is worth f_N(h2) = 38. Theorem R as
stated in `wht35/wider/w-bounds.md` §2 ("the sum of f over the paid steps is at least the sum of f over the targets",
targets per register, so mixed targets are allowed) gives, for a unit with W1 kernel arrays, W2 rank-2 targets (one
spare row) and M rank-1 moves: 20 M ≥ 49 W1 + 38 W2. For (W1, W2, M) = (1, 4, 10): 200 ≥ 201 is false;
(2, 8, 20): 400 ≥ 402 false; (4, 21, 49): 980 ≥ 994 false; (7, 38, 88): 1760 ≥ 1787 false. ∎

**Remarks.**
- The same construction on the 56-dim irreducible (N = its O28 part, dim 28) gives exactly (10, 18, 28) = (80, 144,
  224)/8, the hook (`out_exact_cert2.txt`): the hook and the new certificate are the same object in two irreducibles.
- Scope. The certificate binds the engine game. For unimodular chain maps (class Q) it binds by reps Corollary 1.2's
  route (ut-direct Theorem E holds for any frame family), provided Theorem E is additive over mixed targets as Theorem R
  is [argued]. It does NOT bind the widened game: the widened free group (all address permutations, all Z4 phase
  patterns) does not act through Sp(6,2).
- It does not touch 5 in 14: ψ3/ψ1 = 49/20 = 2.45 < 2.8.


## 2. Theorem S′ (one spectral cap for every cut in every irreducible) [proved]

**Statement.** Let X be an irreducible complex representation of Sp(2m,2) (orthogonal, as all are here), P_0 = Stab(L0),
π_1, …, π_r its P_0-isotypic projections (d_i = rank π_i, D = dim X), h = t_(e1|e1), and M_ij = tr(π_i h π_j h)/d_i the
piece walk of Theorem S. Let k = 2(2^m − 1) and θ1 = 2^m − 3 be the degree and the largest non-trivial eigenvalue of
the frame graph (the dual polar graph DSp(2m,2)). Then

    λ2(M) ≤ θ1/k,

and hence every cut N = ⊕_{i∈S} π_i X (p = dim N/D) has, for every group element g,

    f_N(g)/f_N(h) ≤ 1/((1 − θ1/k)·max(p, 1 − p)) ≤ 2k/(k − θ1) = 4(2^m − 1)/(2^m + 1).

Values: 12/5 (m = 2), 28/9 = 3.111 (m = 3), 60/17 = 3.529 (m = 4), 124/33 = 3.758 (m = 5), 252/65 = 3.877 (m = 6); < 4
for every m. By pf-duality Lemmas 1.2 and 1.5(a) the same cap holds for every certificate in every P_0-multiplicity-free
module.

**Proof.** Take x ∈ R^r with Σ d_i x_i = 0 and put Y = Σ x_i π_i (symmetric, commutes with P_0). Since h is an
orthogonal involution, ½‖[h, Y]‖_F² = tr Y² − tr(YhYh) = Σ d_i x_i² − Σ_ij x_i x_j tr(π_i h π_j h) = ⟨x, (I − M)x⟩_d.
Define F on frames by F(U) = γ_U Y γ_U^{-1} (γ_U L0 = U; well defined because Y commutes with P_0). For adjacent U, V the
element γ_U^{-1}γ_V maps L0 to a neighbour of L0, so lies in P_0 h P_0, and ‖F(U) − F(V)‖² = ‖Y − hYh^{-1}‖² = ‖[h, Y]‖²
(conjugation by orthogonal matrices and by P_0 preserves the norm and Y). The mean of F over frames is the group average
of Y, which is (tr Y/D)·I = 0 by Schur's lemma. The frame graph is k-regular with largest non-trivial eigenvalue θ1, so,
applied coordinatewise, Σ_U Σ_{V∼U} ‖F(U) − F(V)‖² = 2⟨F, (kI − A)F⟩ ≥ 2(k − θ1) Σ_U ‖F(U)‖². The left side is
N_frames·k·‖[h, Y]‖², the right side 2(k − θ1)·N_frames·‖Y‖². Hence ⟨x, (I − M)x⟩_d ≥ (1 − θ1/k)‖x‖_d² on the
d-orthogonal complement of the constants, i.e. λ2 ≤ θ1/k. Insert this in Theorem S (whose proof uses only that N is a sum
of isotypic projections of an irreducible X; its demand bound min(dim N, D − dim N) holds for every g). The eigenvalues
of DSp(2m,2) are θ_j = 2(2^{m−j} − 1) − (2^j − 1) (so θ0 = k = 2(2^m − 1), θ1 = 2^m − 3) [the standard dual polar graph
formula, from memory; checked by direct diagonalisation at m = 2 (6, 1, −3) and m = 3 (14, 5, −1, −7), dpg_eig.py →
out_dpg_eig.txt, and at m = 4 (30, 13, 3, −5, −15), out_fg4.txt]. ∎

**Projective modules.** The proof only conjugates by group elements and uses Schur's lemma, so it holds verbatim for
irreducible projective representations (faithful representations of the double cover 2.Sp(6,2), with P_0 replaced by its
preimage, order 21,504): their cuts are capped by the same 28/9 at block 3. Character-level restrictions of the 13
faithful irreducibles of 2.Sp(6,2) to that preimage: `gap/out_cover.txt` (8, 48, 64, 64 are single pieces; 112, 112, 168
are multiplicity-free with 2-3 pieces; 120, 280, 448, 512, 560, 720 repeat pieces). They were not built as matrices.

**Computed sharpness.** λ2 = θ1/k exactly in 29 of the 30 irreducibles of Sp(6,2) (the 35 with value −5 has −1/14) and in
the three irreducibles 51, 119, 135 of Sp(8,2) (out_cuts_*.txt, out_fg4.txt).

**Consequences.**
- Cuts can never give a θ-consistent family: their ratio stays below 4 at every block, so their θ-shadow
  (spectrum Lemma D) tends to 0. Only certificates with repeated pieces (labels) can matter for the 1e-1 bar at large
  blocks. This generalises "hooks, Venn, coordinate sets cannot" to every multiplicity-free engine certificate.
- A cut beats a target T only if max(p, 1 − p) < 2(2^m − 1)/((2^m + 1)·T): at block 3 and T = 2.8, |p − ½| < 0.056; at
  block 4, T = 10/3 needs |p − ½| < 0.029 and T = 3.5 needs |p − ½| < 0.0042.

## 3. Two facts used for the screen (not new)

- **Mediant and complements.** f_N = f_{N^⊥} for orthogonal actions (rank(π_S g π_{S^c}) = rank((π_{S^c} g^{-1} π_S)ᵀ)),
  so cuts were enumerated up to complement. Direct sums never beat their best summand (pf-duality Lemma 1.2).
- **Theorem S on isotypic cuts.** pf-duality's proof of Theorem S uses only that N is a sum of the isotypic projections
  π_i and that X is irreducible; it applies verbatim to sums of isotypic components in irreducibles with repeated pieces.
  Its per-cut bound also caps ψ2/ψ1 (the demand bound min(dim N, D − dim N) holds for every g), which justified pruning
  cuts with bound ≤ 1.95 < 88/45 in irreducibles of dimension ≥ 280.
