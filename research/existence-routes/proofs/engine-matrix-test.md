## 0. Setting

The engine game as in `../../moonshot/theory-guide/RULES.md` section 0 and `hb.py`: at block m (N = 2^m) the letters
are N x N matrices; `dir z = ca I + cb X^z` (ca = (1+i)/2, cb = (1-i)/2) is paid; the free group B is generated by the
shifts X^z, the address maps (GL(m,2) acting on addresses) and the phase letters `P_{z,c} = diag(i^{(z.x + c) mod 2})`
(this includes the scalar i). G is the group generated by all letters. A register holds `sum_l c_l y_l` with c_l in
C[G]; a free step multiplies a register by an element of B or mixes registers with scalar coefficients; a paid move
multiplies one register by `dir z`. A word is right for every number of digit columns exactly when it is an identity
in C[G] (RULES section 0; exist2 ex2-reform, Burnside-Brauer). A unit "W in M" delivers `K y_l` (K = dir e_1 ... dir
e_m) in W registers, using M moves. With scratch registers, the free steps between two moves realise any matrix over
C[B] (copy, apply letters, add).

Mod Paulis and scalars, G maps onto Sp(2m,2), B onto the parabolic P = Stab(L0) of the frame L0 = {(0,b)}, `dir z`
onto the transvection T_(z,0), and K onto `(x,b) -> (x+b, b)`, which takes L0 to a frame at distance m (`sp.py`).

## Lemma E0 (all moves are alike) [proved]

`dir z = g dir(e_1) g^-1` for an address map g with g e_1 = z (RULES C1: `G dir(z) G^-1 = dir(Gz)`). So each paid
move is `b dir(e_1) b^-1` with b in B, and b, b^-1 can be absorbed into the neighbouring free steps. WLOG every move is
`t := dir e_1`.

*Consequence.* In the engine game the multiset of move directions carries no information. Any test that reads only
the move multiset (the slice's deletion, clique, even and covering tests, the rank-cover LP over direction cells) can
only be a function of (m, W, M). The slice's tests have no engine analogue that sees supports.

## Lemma E1 (engine transfer form) [proved]

Write the word as `Lambda_M D_M ... D_1 Lambda_0`, where the Lambda_k are matrices over C[B] (the free products, of
any shapes) and `D_i = 1 + (t - 1) e_{r_i} e_{r_i}^T` (move i on register r_i). Put
`alpha_i = E_out^T Lambda_M ... Lambda_i e_{r_i}` (W x 1), `beta_i = e_{r_i}^T Lambda_{i-1} ... Lambda_0 E_in` (1 x W),
`gamma_ij = e_{r_j}^T Lambda_{j-1} ... Lambda_i e_{r_i}` (i < j) and `T_0 = E_out^T Lambda_M ... Lambda_0 E_in` (the
word with every move deleted), all over C[B]. Then the delivered W x W matrix over C[G] is

    Z = T_0 + sum over chains i_1 < ... < i_k of alpha_{i_k} (t-1) gamma_{i_{k-1} i_k} (t-1) ... gamma_{i_1 i_2} (t-1) beta_{i_1}.

*Proof.* Expand the product, keeping the order (nothing commutes here). *Converse.* Given any such data over C[B], the
word "u_i := b-part of the inputs + sum_{i'<i} gamma_{i'i} w_{i'}; copy u_i; apply t to the copy; w_i := t u_i - u_i;
output_l := T_0-part + sum_i alpha_{l,i} w_i" realises it, every combination being a matrix over C[B]. []

## Theorem E10 (the bordered rank identity, any representation) [proved; computed]

Let rho be any finite-dimensional representation of G and factor `rho(t) - 1 = P Q^T` with k = rank(rho(t) - 1).
Put `A~ = [rho(alpha_i) P]_i`, `B~ = [Q^T rho(beta_i)]_i`, `G_ji = Q^T rho(gamma_ij) P` (i < j, strictly block lower
triangular) and

    N(lambda) = [[ rho(T_0) - lambda I,  A~ ],
                 [ -B~,                   I - G ]].

Then `rho(Z) = rho(T_0) + A~ (I - G)^{-1} B~`, and for every lambda in C

    rank N(lambda) = M k + rank(rho(Z) - lambda I).

For a unit, `rank N(lambda) = M k + W rank(rho(K) - lambda I)`.

*Proof.* Substituting `t - 1 = P Q^T` in Lemma E1 turns the chain sum into `A~ (sum_n G^n) B~ = A~ (I - G)^{-1} B~`
(G is nilpotent). The Schur complement of the invertible block `I - G` in N(lambda) is
`rho(T_0) - lambda + A~ (I-G)^{-1} B~ = rho(Z) - lambda`. []

*The slice as a special case.* There B is the shifts, every character used is trivial on them, so `rho(T_0) =
eps(T_0) = I` is forced (the trivial character), P = -2 or 0, Q = 1, and the Schur complement with respect to the port
block at lambda = -1 is the slice's E = I + Gamma - K, with Theorem 10's principal ranks.

## Proposition E3 (the rank pattern is blind in the engine game) [proved; computed]

Let `P_K = prod_j P_{e_j,0} = diag(i^{|x|})`, a free letter. Then `K = h P_K h^-1` with
`h = prod_j (S_j dir(e_j) S_j)` in G, where S_j = P_{e_j,0}: indeed `S dir(e_1) S = omega H` (omega = e^{i pi/4}), and
`H^{(x)m} P_K H^{(x)m} = K`. Take M = 0 and `T_0 = P_K I_W`. Then in every representation rho, rho(T_0) is similar to
rho(K) (x) I_W, so

    rank N(lambda) = W rank(rho(P_K) - lambda) = W rank(rho(K) - lambda)   for every rho and lambda.

T_0 is one element of M_W(C[B]), so it also satisfies every coupling between representations through shared
B-components, and `eps(T_0) = I_W` (the trivial representation). Adding idle moves (zero couplings) adds exactly k to
both sides. Hence **every test that depends only on the similarity class of rho(Z) in each representation (ranks,
spectra, Jordan types, the rank pattern of N or of its Schur complements, jointly over any set of representations and
B-components) accepts every (m, W, M), including M = 0.** It cannot even prove that one move is needed. []

[Computed, `out_t_e10.txt`: `rank(rho(P_K) - lambda) = rank(rho(K) - lambda)` for lambda in {1, i, -1, -i} at m = 2,
f = 1..4 and m = 3, f = 1..3; `|H^(x)m P_K H^(x)m - K| = 2e-16`; `S dir(e_1) S / H` constant = omega.]

*Why the slice escapes.* Its free group acts by scalars on the characters it uses, so T_0 is pinned to I in all of
them at once; then K and T_0 are not similar (at odd forms K = -1). In the engine the free group is large enough to
contain a conjugate of K, and similarity data cannot tell them apart. A test with teeth must use the position of B
inside G ("pinning"): compressions by idempotents of C[B], or B-invariant subspaces as Theorem R does.

## Proposition E4 (how many representations share a free component) [proved; computed]

For an irreducible representation sigma of B, the irreducible representations of G that contain sigma are the
constituents of `Ind_B^G sigma`, and `sum_rho (multiplicity of sigma in rho)^2 = dim End_G(Ind_B^G sigma) =
sum over double cosets BgB of dim Hom_{B cap gBg^-1}(sigma, sigma^g)` (Mackey). B\G/B corresponds to P\Sp(2m,2)/P,
i.e. to the frame distance 0..m: m + 1 double cosets [computed, `sp.py` -> `out_sp.txt`: P has exactly m+1 orbits on
the 15, 135, 2295 frames at m = 2, 3, 4, and they are the distance classes]. So:
- the trivial component of B lies in exactly m + 1 irreducible representations, once each (the frame module is
  multiplicity-free: every double coset is closed under inversion, so the Hecke algebra is commutative);
- for the Weil component V|_B every double coset contributes exactly 1 (V stays irreducible on B cap gBg^-1, which
  contains the Paulis, and V^g = V there because V extends to G), so sum_rho mult^2 = m + 1 and V|_B lies in at most
  m + 1 irreducible representations (multiplicity one would need commutativity of this Hecke algebra; not checked).

In the slice, the free group is the shifts and its trivial character lies in 2^(m(m+1)/2) characters (8, 64, 1024 at
m = 2, 3, 4). This is the quantitative form of "thin coupling" in the engine game.

## Theorem E2 (the trivially pinned component) [proved; tightness computed]

Let `e = |B|^-1 sum_{b in B} b`, so `e b = b e = e` and `e alpha e = eps(alpha) e` for alpha in C[B]. Compress the unit
identity on both sides: `e Z e = e K e I_W` in M_W(H), `H = e C[G] e`. H has the basis `u_d = e g_d e` (g_d any element
at frame distance d, d = 0..m); it is commutative with characters psi_0 (trivial), ..., psi_m, and
`psi_j(u_d) = omega_j(d)`, the spherical functions of the dual polar graph of Sp(2m,2) (intersection array
b_i = 2^{i+1}(2^{m-i} - 1), c_i = 2^i - 1, `out_sp.txt`). By Lemma E1,

    psi_j(e Z e) = tau + A R_j B,   R_j = mu_j I + L_j,

where `tau = eps(T_0)`, `A = [eps(alpha_i)]` (W x M), `B = [eps(beta_i)]` (M x W), `mu_j = omega_j(1) - 1` (one move:
`e(t-1)e = u_1 - u_0`), and L_j is strictly lower triangular in time order: its (i, i') entry is psi_j of the sum over
all chains from move i' to move i, an element of H of frame distance at most i - i' + 1 with psi_0 = 0. The component
asks `tau + A R_j B = omega_j(m) I_W` for j = 0..m.

**(i) M >= W + m - 1** (ladder Theorem D; this is theory-guide D2 read in H). The u_m-coefficient of eZe is `A H_m B`
with H_m supported on chains of length >= m, i.e. on entries with i - i' >= m - 1, so rank H_m <= M - m + 1; it must be
a non-zero multiple of I_W.

**(ii) M >= 2W** whenever `c = (omega_j(m) - 1)_{j>=1}` and `mu = (omega_j(1) - 1)_{j>=1}` are not proportional.
*Proof.* j = 0 has mu_0 = 0 and L_0 = 0, so tau = I. For j >= 1, `A R_j B = c_j I_W` with c_j != 0 (|omega_j(m)| < 1),
so A and B have rank W. Choose lambda in C^m with `lambda . c = 0` and `lambda . mu = 1`. Then
`N = sum_j lambda_j R_j = I + (strictly lower triangular)` is invertible and `A N B = 0`. So N maps the W-dimensional
image of B injectively into ker A, whose dimension is M - W. []

*Non-proportionality.* j = 1 and j = m give `c_1/mu_1 = 2 - 2^{1-m}` and `c_m/mu_m = (2/3)(1 - (-1/2)^m)`, which
differ for every m >= 2. This uses `omega_1(1) = (2^m - 3)/(2^{m+1} - 2)`, `omega_1(m) = -2^-m`, `omega_m(1) = -1/2`,
`omega_m(m) = (-1/2)^m` [computed from the intersection array for m = 2..6, `out_sp.txt`; checked in exact rational
arithmetic for m = 2..14, `t_sph_exact.py` -> `out_t_sph_exact.txt`, which verifies that the m+1 distinct numbers
`theta_j = 2(2^{m-j} - 1) - (2^j - 1)` are eigenvalues of the intersection matrix and that both ratios are as stated;
for m > 14 the eigenvalue formula is the classical one for dual polar graphs, memory].

*At block 2* (ii) gives M >= 2W, the plain count: the known closure of the engine game at block 2 (MAP 3.1, A1,
Lean-checked). This is the component's one refusal of a known-impossible case.

*The slice reads the same way.* There `e_X C[A] e_X = C[A/X]` has the 2^(m(m+1)/2) characters chi_Q, with
`mu_Q(z) = (-1)^{Q(z)} - 1` depending on the move's direction and `c_Q = -2 [Q odd]`; the argument of (ii) with lambda
the indicator of an even form, restricted to its support, is Theorem 10(b). So Theorem 10 IS the trivially pinned
component of the slice's own free group. The engine's has only m+1 characters and identical moves.

**(iii) Tightness.**
*Exact, in the abstract model* [proved]. Allow each chain sum to be any element of H of the right distance range
with psi_0 = 0. If W >= m - 1, take M = 2W moves in two blocks of W, `A = [I_W, I_W]`, `B = [I_W; I_W]`, all chain
sums 0 except the one from move l to move W + l (l = 1..W), which is the element h* of H with
`psi_j(h*) = c_j - 2 mu_j` (j = 1..m; psi_0(h*) = 0). Its distance range is W + 1 >= m, so h* is allowed (H has
dimension m + 1 and its characters separate it). Then `A R_j B = 2 mu_j I + h*_j I = c_j I` for every j and tau = I.
So in the abstract model M = 2W is attained whenever W >= m - 1, at every block; in particular 8 arrays in 16 moves at
block 5 and 13 in 26 at block 6.
*Numerical, realised and abstract* [computed]. With M = max(2W, W + m - 1) the component has solutions ("fakes"):
- abstract solutions (A, B, and the chain sums chosen freely inside the distance filtration): `hecke_abs.py`;
- realised solutions, with the inner links gamma actual elements of C[P] (combinations of random elements of P acting
  on the frame module) and the outer couplings eps(alpha), eps(beta), tau: `framefake.py hecke`.
- realised solutions by variable projection (the outer couplings eliminated by least squares): `hecke_vp.py`.
Below the bound the searches stall at residuals 2e-5 .. 1.0, as (i)-(ii) require. Results: `out_hecke_abs_mac.txt`,
`out_framefake_m2.txt` and the Colab outputs listed in NOTE.md. The saved realised solutions at block 3 (2 in 4,
3 in 6) were re-checked by `check_fake.py`, an independent program that writes each one out as an explicit register
word on the frame module and runs it on fresh random complex inputs (errors 1.4e-14, 1.1e-13). By Lemma E1's
converse each realised solution is a word whose output passes the trivially pinned compression.

Realisability of the abstract construction itself is not proved, and is probably false for m >= 3: a real chain sum
from move l to move W + l reaches distance d only through d moves, which here would be other arrays' moves and would
create further non-zero entries. The realised solutions found numerically have a different structure.
*Direct sums* [proved]: two words on disjoint arrays, registers and moves have a block-diagonal Z, so their
compressions do not interact; realised solutions (W1, M1) and (W2, M2) give (W1 + W2, M1 + M2). With the realised
2 in 4 and 3 in 6 at block 3 this covers every W >= 2 at 2 moves per array; with 2 in 5 at block 4, every even W at
2.5. At blocks >= 5 no realised solution was computed. So the trivially pinned component certifies exactly max(2W, W + m - 1) moves where computed: 2 per array, a constant,
already below the hook at block 3 (2.8) and below 2.9447 from block 4 on.

## Proposition E6 (the one-sided trivially pinned component as a submodule game) [proved; computed]

Compressing on one side only, a unit satisfies `M(Z) delta_{L0} = delta_{K L0} (x) I_W` in the frame module
M = C[frames] (Pauli-blind level; M(beta) delta_{L0} = eps(beta) delta_{L0} because P fixes L0). By Lemma E1, the
left side is `tau delta_{L0} + sum_i M(alpha_i) w_i`, with `w_i = (M(t) - 1) u_i` and
`u_i = delta_{L0} b_i + sum_{i'<i} M(gamma_{i'i}) w_{i'}`. Here the alpha_i and gamma range over all of C[P], acting
on the frame index of (C^n)^W. Hence some word with M moves satisfies the one-sided condition iff the following game
can be won:
`V_0 = delta_{L0} (x) C^W`; move i picks any u_i in V_{i-1} and sets `V_i = V_{i-1} + C[P] (M(t) - 1) u_i`;
win iff `delta_{K L0} (x) e_l` lies in V_M for every l.
The P-averaged part of V_M is spanned by V_0 and the class sums of the w_i, which is exactly the two-sided condition
(Theorem E2). So the one-sided condition is "two-sided, plus the non-trivial isotypic parts of V_M contain those of
C[O_m]^W".

## Remark E7 (where Theorem R's frame certificates live) [read]

The Averaging Lemma (`wht35/wider/w-bounds.md` section 3) builds its certificate in `Map(Gamma, V_0)`, Gamma the frame
permutation group (= Sp(2m,2)), with the kept subspace `{(v_gamma) : v_gamma in Y_{gamma^-1 L0}}`. That is the regular
representation of Sp(2m,2) (tensored with V_0), which contains every Pauli-blind component. It is not inside the
trivial component's m+1 representations. The hook's pinning is a kept subspace across many components at once.

## Remark E5 (higher components) [argued]

For a B-irreducible sigma of dimension d > 1 with primitive idempotent e_sigma, `e_sigma alpha` is not a scalar
multiple of e_sigma; in the two-sided compression each move contributes d slots instead of one, and the argument of
E2(ii) gives only `M d >= 2W`. The full family of compressions `e_sigma Z e_sigma'` over all pairs is equivalent to the
unit identity itself, so the hierarchy as a whole has no ceiling below the truth; but no single compression analysed
here goes beyond 2 per array.
