## 0. The two questions and the answers

1. *Does a repaired duality hold?* Yes, but not "per width". The composition semiring S of meta-spectrum has no power
   universal element, so neither [F18] nor [F20] applies to S as it stands (Lemma 1; whether Fritz's part II applies,
   through a power universal pair, I could not settle). The width bookkeeping is the problem, not the mathematics: in the job model that the engine's recursion
   actually is (ex-laser 1.2: registers always have spare rows), lower boundedness holds globally, and the job semiring J
   is **Strassen preordered** (Lemma 2, with a new pipelining lemma for transitivity). Strassen's theorem and Fritz's
   strict form then hold for J (Theorem 3), and J's spectrum is {n -> n^theta : theta in Theta}, with no degenerate points.
   Per block, the ordered-monoid rate formula [F17, Thm 8.24] gives an exact per-block duality (Proposition 7, [argued]).
2. *Does it yield one finite unit, given the Phi barrier?* Its conclusions ARE single finite units (x^n >= y^n exactly,
   for one fixed n), and the Phi barrier does not forbid them: the Phi barrier is literally the statement that the
   spectral point phi_tau is monotone on the sub-semiring generated by the rules used, i.e. the "only if" half of this
   same duality, and Theorem 1 is its constructive "if" half for rank-1-move rules. **But** the hypothesis that produces
   a unit beating exponent theta_0 is "no spectral point above theta_0", which holds only if finitely many relations of J
   (units of some kind) already violate every phi_theta with theta >= theta_0 (Closure Lemma 4). So the duality never
   creates a saving: from the known units it reaches exactly the known exponent. As an existence method for the bar it
   is **blind** (calibration, section 5).
3. *What it does give* (by-products, all unchecked): (a) every relation of J, whatever its shape (mixed targets, grouped
   paid blocks, fractional portions, additive or multiplicative catalysts), is worth exactly the largest theta at which
   its Phi-balance is still >= 0 (Corollary 5: this settles ex-laser's open "Theorem 1 for grouped paid blocks" at the
   level of exponents, non-constructively); (b) **the exponent of a SET of relations is the largest theta in the
   intersection of their Theta-sets, so a relation that saves nothing at plain prices can amplify a saving one**
   (Corollary 6), a laser-type effect that ex-note 2.3 says has no analogue here. It needs paid blocks larger than some
   target (two sign changes of the Phi-balance). The fluid LP confirms the effect on hypothetical rules (section 6).
   (c) The smallest such relation, the "spreading" mixed unit 2[2] <= [1] + [3] (two arrays owed 2 of 3 rows, one rank-1
   and one rank-3 paid block), would, with any saving unit, give saving -> 1 (Corollary 6(c)). It passes the block-3
   hook, Theorem D and the 2-adic point, but it is **excluded** by w-bounds' symmetric certificate A (delta 4, 6, 7:
   4 + 7 < 2 x 6), and the whole spreading family 2[k] <= [k-j] + [k+j] is excluded at every block by the
   label-permutation certificate, whose price 1 - 2^-d is strictly concave (section 6, end) [derived here].

## 1. S as it stands has no Vergleichsstellensatz

**Lemma 1** [proved for the element; open for the pair]. The composition semiring S (elements sum W_i [m_i], arrays of
width m_i, [m][k] = [mk]) has no power universal element ([F20, Def. 3.27], equivalently [F20, Lemma 3.29]: u >= 1 with
x <= u^k and 1 <= x u^k for every nonzero x). Hence [F18, Thm 2.12] and [F20, Thm 7.15] do not apply to S.
*Proof.* Every element of u^k [m] (m >= 2) is a sum of oracles of width >= m (products multiply widths), and no program
serves a width-1 task from oracles of width >= 2 (spectrum A6). So 1 <= [m] u^k fails for every u, k. ∎
*Not settled:* whether S has a power universal PAIR (u-, u+) ([F20, Def. 3.27(b)]; the polynomial growth of Fritz's
part II, arXiv:2112.05949). The width argument only rules out pairs whose u- has a width-1 summand; the candidate
(u-, u+) = ([2], 1 + [2]) survives it, and the cases I tried (x = [8] <= y = 8[1]: y [2^k] <= x (1 + [2])^k for k >= 3
by Kronecker tiling) satisfy the condition [argued, not checked for all x <= y]. If it holds, part II's
Vergleichsstellensatz (real, tropical and derivation points) applies to S. This changes nothing below: the job
semiring J of section 2 is Archimedean, is the faithful model of the recursion, and the circularity of section 4 is a
property of every such duality here (its dual side is cut out by the relations themselves).
(The width point t_1 = 1, t_n = 0 of meta-spectrum is a monotone homomorphism with nonzero kernel, which [F20] shows
cannot occur under polynomial growth: consistent.) Vrana's locally compact generalisation (arXiv:2003.14176) relaxes
boundedness [read: abstract only]; I did not check whether S fits it. It is not needed: section 2 removes the cause.

## 2. The job semiring J is Strassen preordered

**Definition.** J: finite formal sums x = sum_i w_i [s_i], w_i in Q_{>=0}, s_i >= 1. [s] is a *job of s rows*: at column
count c, H on s chosen rows (s c bits) of a register that has as many further spare rows as needed (ex-laser 1.1-1.2:
"spare rows come from the register's other bits"); the weight is the fraction of the register's entries. Sum: side by
side. Product: [a][b] = [ab] (rows of b-row bundles). A *program for x >= y* is a straight-line word of engine free
steps (Q(i) mixing of equal-shaped portions; Paulis, affine maps, the engine's phases on any rows, column-uniform),
correct for every column count, that completes the jobs of y (given simultaneously, each on its own register portion)
calling jobs of total weight <= w_s at each size s. **Preorder:** x >= y iff for every eps > 0 there is a program for
(1+eps)x >= y. 1 = [1], 0 = nothing.

**Lemma 2** [proved, except (b) for the engine's phase letters, which is spectrum A2's [argued] step].
(a) >= is a preorder (transitivity: Pipelining Lemma below).
(b) x >= y implies x + z >= y + z and xz >= yz (side by side; regrouping columns as in A2).
(c) 1 >= 0, and N -> J is an order embedding: n[1] >= n'[1] implies n >= n'.
(d) For every nonzero x there is l in N with l x >= 1 and l >= x.
So J is a Strassen preordered semiring [F18, Def. 2.4], a Q_+-semialgebra ([F18, Remark 2.15]).
*Proof of (c).* The frame-count certificate of spectrum A3 (Y_0 = line of the all-Z frame L_0 of the register's rows,
fixed by every engine free letter; every job, call or task, moves L_0): Theorem R gives total call weight >= total task
weight. *Proof of (d).* [s] <= s[1] (plain); [1] <= 2[m] for every m by spectrum A5 applied to the task row and m - 1
spare rows: dir = S_1 K S_1 K^-1 S_1^3 with K = H on those m rows, K^-1 = K X_all (checked exactly by
`../../meta/spectrum/archimedes.py`, m = 1, 2, 3, all m by tensoring). For x with a summand w[m], (2/w) x >= 1. ∎

**Pipelining Lemma** [proved]. If P is a program for x >= y and Q a program for y >= z, then for every eps > 0 there is
a program for (1+eps)x >= z.
*Proof.* Q issues its calls c_1, ..., c_L (the jobs of y) in order. Split every register of z into N equal portions
(a free relabelling of spare rows); copy i is Q run on the i-th portions. In round t = 1, ..., N + L - 1, every copy i
with 1 <= t - i + 1 <= L runs its free steps up to its call number t - i + 1 and leaves that call pending; its inputs
come from its own earlier rounds. For L <= t <= N the pending calls are exactly one call of each number 1..L, i.e. one
copy of y scaled by 1/N, available simultaneously: P (scaled by 1/N) serves them with x/N, and returns before round
t + 1. In the 2(L - 1) boundary rounds, serve each pending call [q] directly by the plain word and A5 (one straight-line
substitution, no composition needed): at most 4 q calls of a size present in x, weight 1/N each. Total: x(N - L + 1)/N
+ O(L q_max / N) x-sized calls <= (1 + eps) x for N large. ∎
(This is the general form of ex-laser's lockstep Lemma 2: staggering copies makes any multiset of sequential calls
simultaneous. Free passes are not counted by J; they cost a constant factor in the realised recursion, as in Theorem 1.)

## 3. The repaired duality

**Theorem 3** [proved, given Lemma 2]. (i) Every monotone semiring homomorphism phi: J -> R_+ is phi_theta([s]) = s^theta
(extended Q_+-linearly) for one theta in [0, 1]. Let Theta = {theta : phi_theta monotone}; it is closed, 0 in Theta, and
theta* := max Theta. (ii) For nonzero x, y in J: phi_theta(x) >= phi_theta(y) for all theta in Theta iff for every eps
there are k <= eps n with 2^k x^n >= y^n [F18, Thm 2.6 / Cor. 2.18]. (iii) If phi_theta(x) > phi_theta(y) for all theta
in Theta and x is power universal (e.g. x = c 1 with c >= 2, by [F20, Lemma 3.29] and Lemma 2(d)), then x^n >= y^n for
all n >> 1 [F20, Thm 7.15(d), with Fritz's convention reversed]. (iv) In particular, for N >= 2 and rational c:
c > N^theta* implies c^n 1 >= [N^n] for all n >> 1; c < N^theta* implies c^n 1 >= [N^n] for no n.
*Proof.* (i) Theorem C's proof uses only Kronecker [a+b] <= [a] + [b] and plain [n] <= n[1], both in J; its degenerate
branch (all t_n <= 1, some t_a < 1) is impossible here: 2 t_m >= 1 by Lemma 2(d), while t_{a^j} = t_a^j -> 0.
Q_+-linearity: [F18, Remark 2.15]. Monotone theta form a closed set (pointwise limits of monotone maps are monotone);
0 is monotone by Lemma 2(c). (ii), (iii): the cited theorems, whose hypotheses are Lemma 2. (iv): phi_theta(c) = c. ∎
A relation c^n 1 >= [N^n] is a fixed finite program: H on N^n rows with (1+eps)c^n single-row calls. Used recursively
(products) it gives cost per entry (log of the size)^(log_N c) for all sizes. Spare rows: the program needs a register
lambda times wider than its task for some fixed lambda; a first Kronecker split of the top job into lambda pieces
provides that at a constant factor (the top split of ex-laser 1.3). So the exponent is exactly theta*.

## 4. Closure: the duality never creates a saving

For a relation rho in J (tasks {k: w_k} served by calls {r: p_r}) put D_rho(theta) = sum p_r r^theta - sum w_k k^theta
and Theta_rho = {theta in [0,1] : D_rho(theta) >= 0}. For a set R of relations let J_R be J with the preorder generated by
R and the base relations (plain, Kronecker, A5; all monotone for every theta in [0,1]). J_R is Strassen preordered (its
order lies inside J's, so (c) holds; (d) uses only base relations; transitivity and compatibility hold by construction,
and every generated relation is realisable by the Pipelining Lemma).

**Lemma 4 (closure)** [proved]. Theta(J_R) = intersection over rho in R of Theta_rho. Hence the best exponent reachable
from R is tau(R) := max of that intersection, and it is reached (Theorem 3(iv) in J_R).
*Proof.* phi_theta is a semiring homomorphism; it is monotone on the generated preorder iff it is monotone on the
generators (sums, products, transitivity and the eps-closure all preserve "phi(x) >= phi(y)"). ∎
**Consequences.** (1) The Phi barrier (Theorem 3 of ex-laser) is exactly "phi_tau is monotone on J_R": cost >= n^tau for
every tau in Theta(J_R). (2) From the known units, the duality gives exponent tau(known) = 1 - 1.67e-3 at today's pricing (lower only through
relations derived from the same units, e.g. truncation if its hypothesis holds), nothing more.
(3) theta* < theta_0 iff Theta(J) misses [theta_0, 1] iff (compactness) finitely many relations of J have Phi-balances
that are negative somewhere and jointly cover [theta_0, 1]. A duality proof of "saving >= 0.1" therefore needs those
relations, i.e. it needs the very objects whose existence is in question. That is the obstruction.

## 7. The per-block duality (the "per width" repair the brief asked about)

**Proposition 7** [argued]. Let A_m be the per-block monoid of meta-spectrum with spare rows (so the Pipelining Lemma
gives transitivity up to eps, which is the regularised order of [F17]). Every element is >= 0 (unused calls are
dropped) and dir is a generator (n dir >= x for every x, the group being generated by directions and free letters), so
[F17, Thm 8.24] applies: the best amortised number of moves per array at block m equals the supremum over ALL additive
monotones psi of A_m of psi(K)/psi(dir).
*Use.* It turns certificate ceilings into dichotomies, not into existence. Example: pf-duality's ceiling for all
Theorem-R certificates at block 3 is 169/57 = 2.965 [unchecked]; so either amortised block-3 units with at most
2.965 + eps moves per array exist (saving >= 0.0107), or some additive monotone of A_3 is not a Theorem-R certificate.
The dual side is again cut out by the unknown units: to bound ALL monotones one needs relations (circular), or a
completeness theorem for a computable class, which nobody has.
