# The smallest odd-order separators

**What it shows.** Among combinations of at most three terms of the order-3 group algebra with coefficients 1, -1, i,
-i, it lists those that are invertible over the complex numbers and singular modulo 2 (the kind a separating step needs:
undoing them divides by 2), with their exact inverses. The smallest are 1 + sigma and 1 + sigma - sigma^2, both with
denominator 2.

**Command.** `python3 sep3.py` (standard library only). Under a second.

**Expected output.** `out_sep3.txt`.
