package/lever: the recursion lever on the community's #3945 circuit, saving 1.7501109e-3

What it is
  The new Lean files of this repository's result: OpenAI's statement (the Fourier transform of every length, and
  convolution) with the exponent 1 - 17501109/10^10. Laid over Ryan Shea's tree after package/u8x0hq
  (result.json, "requires"). Final theorems, in Work.FourierL3945.Main:
    OAI.PowerSaving.transform_mainL3945 (the transform of every length)
    OAI.PowerSaving.convolution_mainL3945 (convolution)
